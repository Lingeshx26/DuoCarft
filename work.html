AJ Travels — Business Card Design

Rentals & Acting Service · Velayuthampalayam, Karur

Print design

AJ Travels needed a professional business card to hand out to customers — something that covered their full range of services while still looking clean enough to fit in a wallet. We designed a two-sided card: the front carries the brand and contact details, the back lists every service they offer.

  • Front side — logo, tagline, phone numbers, and location
  • Back side — full service list (family functions, temple trips, outstation, airport pickup, car delivery, tours, and more), plus a 24/7 availability badge
  • Print-ready file, delivered ready to hand to a printer
