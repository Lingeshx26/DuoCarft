AJ Travels — Business Card Design

Rentals & Acting Service · Velayuthampalayam, Karur

Print design

AJ Travels needed a professional business card to hand out to customers — something that covered their full range of services while still looking clean enough to fit in a wallet. We designed a two-sided card: the front carries the brand and contact details, the back lists every service they offer.

  • Front side — logo, tagline, phone numbers, and location
  • Back side — full service list (family functions, temple trips, outstation, airport pickup, car delivery, tours, and more), plus a 24/7 availability badge
  • Print-ready file, delivered ready to hand to a printer

Gig Driver Dashboard — Personal Tool

Built by DuoCraft, for GIG driving work.

Web app · PWA

Built to solve a real problem while riding for Rapido and Blinkit — a phone-first tracker that logs every ride's platform, pickup and drop points, fare, tips, and payment mode, with day-level KM and fuel tracking that adds up automatically. Everything syncs live to Google Sheets, so the data's always there to review.

  • GPS-tagged pickup & drop points, with route distance calculated automatically between them
  • Day and ride history — week/month/all-time totals, best pickup zone, best platform, tips collected
  • Installable as a PWA, works offline for in-progress rides, syncs to Google Sheets via Apps Script
